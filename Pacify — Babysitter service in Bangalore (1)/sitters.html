Your safety comes first

Pacify house rules.

  1. 01A woman of the house is present

    For at-home sessions, an adult woman of the household stays home throughout.

  2. 02Video call before every first booking

    You always see who you’re meeting before you go.

  3. 03Public places for outings

    Malls, cafés and other public spaces, only where you feel comfortable.

  4. 04Children aged 2 and up

    You care only for the number of children agreed in the booking.

  5. 05We’re a call away

    If anything feels off, you can end the session and call us. We’ll take it from there.

  6. 06Your details stay private

    Your number and address are shared only after a booking is confirmed.

  7. 07You’re there to play and care

    No housework, cooking, driving or giving medicine. Just the little ones.

Sitter verification

Five simple checks.

Parents trust Pacify because every sitter goes through the same checks. We guide you through each step.

01

Identity & address

Your Aadhaar card. That’s it.

02

Police verification

A Police Clearance Certificate (PCC). Never got one before? We’ll walk you through it.

03

Qualification check

Your college or university ID, or your latest degree certificate.

04

Psychologist screening

A short, friendly chat with our verified psychologist to understand how you connect with children. No right or wrong answers.

05

Health declaration

A simple self-declaration that you don’t have any contagious illness that could affect children.
