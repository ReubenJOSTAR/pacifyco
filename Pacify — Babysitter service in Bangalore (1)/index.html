FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What babysitting services in Bangalore does Pacify offer?
We offer two services: a babysitter at home for a few hours, and out-and-about babysitting, where a sitter joins you at a mall or café. Both are part-time, so you only book the hours you need.
How much does a babysitter in Bangalore cost?
Babysitting charges at Pacify start from ₹225 per hour per child and upwards, plus a small platform fee. We keep pricing simple and transparent, so families can find affordable babysitters without compromising on safety.
How do I find a babysitter near me?
Join our waitlist and tell us where you live. We'll match you with local babysitters from your part of Bangalore, share their profiles, and set up a one-on-one interview before you confirm.
Can I hire a babysitter for just a few hours?
Yes. Pacify is built for part-time care, whether that's an afternoon to yourself, a work call, or an evening out with your partner.
Can I book a babysitter for weekends or evenings?
Yes. Weekends and evenings are often when parents need a break the most, so you can request a weekend babysitter or an evening sitter for date night.
How do you verify your babysitters?
Every Pacify sitter goes through identity and address checks, police verification, a qualification check, a one-on-one psychologist screening, a health clearance from a doctor, and first aid and CPR training for children. You also meet your sitter before your first booking.
What age groups do your sitters look after?
Our sitters care for toddlers and children aged 2 years and above. We don't currently offer care for babies under 2.
Can one sitter look after more than one child?
Yes. Let us know how many children need care, including children from neighboring families, and we'll plan the right support. Pricing is per child.
Do you offer a full-time babysitter in Bangalore or live-in nannies?
Not at the moment. Pacify focuses on part-time babysitting, so you get flexible, trusted help for the hours you actually need it.
How is Pacify different from a babysitter agency or other babysitting websites?
Agencies mostly place full-time nannies, and most babysitting websites simply list profiles for you to sort through. Pacify focuses on part-time care: we call every family, hand-pick sitter matches, and arrange a one-on-one interview before you book.
What if I need to cancel?
Please cancel at least 8 hours before your booking so your sitter can plan their day.
