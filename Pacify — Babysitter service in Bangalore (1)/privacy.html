Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Pacifyco ("we", "us") helps families in Bengaluru find trusted babysitters. This policy explains what information we collect through www.pacifyco.in, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you join our waitlist as a parent, or sign up as a sitter, we ask for:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your area or locality in Bengaluru

If you call, email or message us, we also keep the details you share in that conversation. Our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical data (such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit) to keep the site running and secure.

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this website.

How we use it

  • To contact you about joining Pacifyco and to let you know when we launch in your area.
  • To match parents with suitable sitters nearby.
  • For sitters, to begin our screening and verification process.
  • To answer your questions and improve our service.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Who we share it with

We share information only where needed to run the service:

  • Service providers that help us operate, such as EmailJS (which delivers form submissions to our inbox) and our website hosting provider.
  • Parents and sitters you are matched with, limited to what is needed to arrange a booking.
  • Authorities, if we are required to by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you are on our waitlist or use Pacifyco, and delete it when it is no longer needed or when you ask us to.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, but we work to keep your details safe.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Just email or call us using the details below and we will respond promptly, in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

Our forms are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
